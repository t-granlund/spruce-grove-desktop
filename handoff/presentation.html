I · the lineage

You built the reference shape

Mockingbird — local-first voice dictation, meeting capture, a knowledge engine. Zero telemetry. Tauri 2. Whisper on-device. When we needed a desktop shell for the grove, the README says it plainly: "Mockingbird is the reference shape."

what we kept

The thin-shell discipline. No agent logic in the app. Local-first. Zero telemetry. A webview that respects the machine.

what we adapted

Instead of re-implementing the harness, the shell drives the groomed CLI — every prompt spawns spruce-grove headless and streams back.

what stayed yours

The temperament: a working animal that thinks out loud, takes dictation, and never phones home.

II · what it is

A thin shell with four contracts

01 · spawn + stream

grove_send spawns spruce-grove --prompt … in the chosen working dir, headless. Stdout streams to the transcript as it lands.

02 · resume

Turns 2+ run with --quick-resume — the conversation keeps its notes across turns.

03 · cancel

One active run, one cancel button. The grove forgives a change of mind.

04 · look-in

The live browser panel: screenshots and actions while the agent works. Trust through seeing.

The shell owns no agent logic. The fork's diff never grows. Your job is the part only you can do: the experience.

V · the laws

GRAN, for the desktop

The canvas is never flat black.Two radials: moss high, ember low-corner. Depth before content.
Grain on everything dark.3–6% opacity, overlay blend. The xerox soul, executed modern.
Four type voices.Fraunces monumental · JetBrains Mono machine · EB Garamond reading · IM Fell ceremony. Never more.
Three symbols only.The grove mark, the North Star, the algiz rune. No emoji. Ever.
Motion is scarce.Glow, fade, the draw-on. Nothing bounces. Restraint is the rebellion.

Speak the task.The plain sentence is the interface — in the tool, and in the room.
